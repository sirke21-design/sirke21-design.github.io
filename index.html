    Featured Project · 2024–2026

    Meeting Assistant

    A local-first Power Apps workbench that consolidates the entire customer meeting lifecycle — prep, execution, follow-up — into a single workspace with AI assistants grounded in JAGGAER's product knowledge.

    The problem

    Customer Success Managers at JAGGAER lose hours each week jumping between Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, Jira, and four different product knowledge bases just to prepare for, run, and follow up on a single customer meeting. Context-switching reduces meeting quality and delays follow-up — both of which directly impact retention.

    The solution

    A Power Apps workbench that runs locally on each CSM's machine, pulls from their own connected Microsoft tools, and uses purpose-built AI assistants — one per JAGGAER product line — to generate talking points, draft notes from transcripts, and schedule follow-ups without leaving the app.

    Power Apps · Power Automate · Copilot Studio · Microsoft Graph · Outlook · Teams · SharePoint · Jira

    Local-first

    App data stays on each CSM's machine. Only meeting notes export to SharePoint, and only on explicit user action. No centralized telemetry.

    Bot-per-product

    Separate Copilot Studio bots for ASO, JA, JD, JI. Narrow scope per bot dramatically reduces hallucination vs. one general bot.

    User-permissioned

    Every connector runs under the user's own Microsoft credentials. The app never holds elevated permissions or scrapes data the user can't already see.
