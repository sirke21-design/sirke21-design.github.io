How I work

01

Real pain over abstract requirements.

I build for workflows I run myself. If I can't describe the friction in one sentence, I don't build for it.

02

Narrow bots, not general ones.

A bot scoped to one product line beats a general assistant on accuracy. Hallucination control is an architecture choice, not a prompt tweak.

03

User-permissioned, local-first.

Where possible, tools run on the user's own permissions and keep data local until the user exports it.

04

Cross-check across models.

I triangulate outputs across Claude and ChatGPT before shipping. A single-model output is a first draft.

05

Ship to one user, then roll wider.

Power Platform lets me prototype in days. One CSM gets it first, I fix what breaks, then it rolls out to the whole team.

Featured build · JAGGAER · 2025 – present

Meeting Assistant

A local-first Power Apps workbench, running inside Microsoft Teams, that pulls the whole customer-meeting lifecycle - prep, execution, follow-up - into one workspace, with AI assistants grounded in JAGGAER's product knowledge.

~30%less time on meeting work: prep, live questions, notes and follow-up
28users at peak, across the CS department
5+apps a CSM no longer switches between
4product-specific bots: ASO · JA · JD · JI

The problem

CSMs lose hours every week jumping between Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, Jira and four product knowledge bases just to prepare for, run and follow up on one customer meeting. Context-switching lowers meeting quality and delays follow-up - and both put retention at risk.

The solution

A workbench in Teams that loads each CSM's meetings, builds talking points, drafts the recap with AI and sends it to every attendee in one click - with purpose-built assistants, one per product line, a click away.

Design choices

Local-first

Built in Power Apps, with Copilot Studio for the AI. All data is written locally on the CSM's machine. The only outbound actions are meeting invites and updates sent through Outlook and Teams to you and the other attendees.

Bot-per-product

Separate Copilot Studio bots for ASO, JA, JD and JI. A narrow scope per bot dramatically reduces hallucination compared with one general bot.

User-permissioned

Every connector runs under the user's own Microsoft credentials. The app never holds elevated permissions or reaches data the user can't already see.

Why it isn't just another meeting tool

Built by a CSM, not a developer.

The workflow logic comes from running customer meetings every day for years. Every screen exists because something specific was slowing me down.

Routed, not generic.

Most "AI assistants" use one general model and hope for the best. This one routes each question to a narrow, knowledge-grounded bot, so answers stay accurate inside each product's domain.
